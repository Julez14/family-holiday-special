# Continuity, compilation and recovery

Each accepted individual portrait receives a stable character ID and an owned source-asset ID. A normalized generous crop includes hair and distinctive accessories. The confirmed crop is the photographic identity anchor. The full normalized source stays private for owner preview and recropping and is blocked from real-mode inference URLs. GPT Image creates one canonical waist-up cartoon per person; its asset ID and wardrobe are persisted and reused rather than redrawn independently for every scene.

The preset compiler is ordinary deterministic TypeScript. It takes the saved cast, descriptions, preset and variation seed and emits a validated manifest. One person appears in every shot; two or three rotate through solo/paired shots; four to six are covered in the first three shots, in groups of no more than two. A fourth shot supplies the payoff. All eighteen preset/cast-size combinations are tested. Every moving shot has a single readable action, a concrete repeated setting, a fixed framing and versioned 2D style guidance. Names never become generated image lettering. Custom text is quoted performance guidance and cannot choose endpoints, URLs, shot counts or infrastructure actions.

Starting images receive both confirmed cropped identity references and accepted canonical looks. H3 receives the corresponding starting image and canonical subject references. Seed reuse is a variation aid, not an identity guarantee. Likeness must still be judged by people who know the subjects.

A Workflow persists each stage. Before a queue submission it creates the attempt and reserves conservative spend, then atomically claims a concurrency slot. The submission step has no automatic retry. A callback can assign a receipt before the submission handler saves it; a durable inbox records its request ID and result. Repeated and reordered deliveries never start another stage. Polling queries a known request if a callback is absent. Lost acceptance without a receipt remains unknown, retains its reservation, and never causes a blind second paid call.

Only a known technical/transient failure can consume a repair allowance. Accepted artifact IDs are reused; a failed clip does not recreate the cast or the other clips. A project has at most two extra image attempts and one extra video attempt. Safety rejections, unknown submissions and budget failures are terminal. Advisory vision errors or `uncertain` do not spend on repair; enable clear-failure judgments only after calibration. There is no user scene-retry or post-completion regeneration endpoint.

FFmpeg strips audio from every source clip while normalizing/encoding four 4.5-second segments. Pillow composes a two-second end card from all selected canonical portraits. The final mux maps only that picture and the selected instrumental music bed, producing H.264/yuv420p, 1280×720, 24 fps with one AAC stream. A real adversarial-tone test verifies the audio isolation. Publish updates the render and project atomically only after checks; late results cannot revive a deleted project.

For measurement, each attempt keeps an observed submission timestamp, the first observed `IN_PROGRESS` time, completion time and provider-reported inference milliseconds when available. Polling observations include polling/network delay and are not exact provider queue measurements; absent metrics remain null. Phase transition events measure the wider pipeline separately. Share-page fetches, recipient playback and create clicks are separate aggregate events, so link copying does not masquerade as distribution.
